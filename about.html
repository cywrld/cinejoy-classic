Our idea

Old films.
New eyes.

Cywrld is a small, independent guide to early and classic cinema: the experiments, the bold visual worlds, the comic timing, and the ideas that still feel alive.

We believe a good introduction should feel welcoming, not like homework. So we pair concise film notes with a simple way to explore, compare, and save what catches your eye.
